Scheduling Algorithms

FCFS — First Come First Serve

Processes are executed in the order they arrive. Simple but can cause long waiting times for later processes (convoy effect). Non-preemptive.

SJF — Shortest Job First

The process with the smallest burst time runs first. Minimizes average waiting time but requires knowing burst times in advance. Non-preemptive.

Round Robin

Each process gets a fixed time slice (quantum). After its quantum expires, it goes to the back of the queue. Fair for all processes. Good for interactive systems.

Priority Scheduling

High-priority processes get more CPU time. Can lead to starvation of low-priority processes. Our system prevents starvation by boosting priority after long wait times.
